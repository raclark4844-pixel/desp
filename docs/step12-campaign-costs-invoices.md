# Campaign records, operating costs and weekly invoices

Employee pages: `/campaign-history`, `/campaign-history/[id]`, `/campaign-costs`, `/billing`, `/billing/[id]`. All require active employee sign-in; cost/rate/email-setting changes also require administrator access and same-origin requests. Customer profile/directory and Operations link to the new pages. Customer campaigns stay in the existing shared Neon database with their customerId/campaignId/leadId lineage; they are presented as separate customer records, not separate physical databases.

History groups campaigns by customer and sorts newest first by first actual job start, using creation dates for campaigns not yet run. It shows campaign and customer totals for lead handoffs whose SMS provider accepted the send. A campaign record contains lead summaries, send states and execution history. These are customer lead deliveries, not counts of prospects contacted.

Costs: the existing Cost ledger stores admin-entered USD expenses, with campaign/customer linkage, date, category, provider, description, creator and request ID. Submission retries cannot duplicate an expense. Call fees, number rental, hosting and setup can use Other. Observed usage estimates use administrator-configured rates saved on each campaign; unknown rates remain unknown. Data/enrichment counts represent job attempts; AI counts requests; SMS/email counts messages; calls count accepted/unknown attempts. These are planning estimates, not automatically reconciled provider charges. SMS segments, data records, token usage, call minutes, failures and subscription allocations can differ. Do not sum estimated costs with actual ledger costs. No vendor prices are invented.

Billing: each previously unbilled LEAD_HANDOFF in SENT state becomes one $60 USD invoice line. PENDING/FAILED/UNKNOWN and generic employee reply alerts are excluded. Unique customer/cutoff, notification, and campaign/lead keys plus the queue lock prevent duplicate invoices and billing. Prepared invoices snapshot the customer name, recipients, lead summaries, per-lead price and total in integer cents. Customer invoices do not pass through operating costs. No tax, late fees, payment terms or payment collection are invented.

Schedule: the requested Friday midnight is interpreted as end of Friday, Saturday 00:00 America/New_York. Vercel calls `/api/internal/billing/worker` every five minutes using CRON_SECRET. The handler computes the last completed cutoff with daylight-saving awareness, creates invoices for up to 20 eligible customers per invocation, and sends one ready invoice. Larger queues drain over successive runs. This is scheduled processing at/after midnight, not a guarantee of inbox arrival exactly at midnight. It catches up after downtime and includes previously unbilled older sends; no empty invoices are generated. Missing customer email or paused billing is shown in the UI. Adding an email later allows catch-up. Default invoicing is enabled for active customers per the user's request.

Recipients: customer invoice email (or Customer.contactEmail), ryan@demoretechnologysolutions.com, and an optional global additional recipient entered later on Invoices. De-duplicate equal addresses. Recipients are frozen on created invoices; changes affect future records. Invoice emails are plain text with the campaign/lead summaries and quantity × $60 total. Email bodies contain the lead details already shared with that customer. Only employees can open invoice pages; customers receive the actual summary by email and need no app login.

Email setup required in production: RESEND_API_KEY, BILLING_FROM on a verified sending domain, and BILLING_EMAIL_VERIFIED=true only after sender/DNS verification. Subscribe the existing signed `/api/outreach/resend` webhook to delivered, bounced, complained, failed and suppressed events and set RESEND_WEBHOOK_SECRET. Sent means accepted by the provider; Delivered requires callback. Bounces/complaints/failures pause future invoicing for that customer for review. The worker uses an invoice-specific idempotency key, claims before sending, and never blindly retries ambiguous outcomes. Stale SENDING becomes UNKNOWN. Review FAILED/UNKNOWN in the email provider before any manual resend; this release has no automatic collection or resend button.

Validation: 27 unit tests include Eastern/DST boundaries and endpoint protection; isolated billing tests cover sent-only counts, customer isolation, concurrent duplicate protection, recipient snapshots, $60 math, cost entry idempotency, rate estimates and ambiguous-email behavior. No real emails, texts or calls are sent in tests. Migration adds billing tables; existing customers/campaigns/leads remain intact.
